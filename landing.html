Ventures & Robotics

An overview of active hardware initiatives, industrial exploration,
and competitive automation systems design.

Core Focus Areas

I focus on translating abstract logic into deterministic mechanical motion and hardware operations. By connecting embedded systems firmware, custom schematic routing, and deliberate mechanical linkages, I build projects designed to perform reliably under challenging constraints. Below are the primary hubs where my engineering work and research are actively deployed.

HELIZON

Founded as an independent technology and innovation vehicle, HELIZON serves as the primary incubator for my scientific and engineering concepts. Its goal is to design functional, minimalist tech ecosystems and embedded infrastructure designed for long-term integration.

Vortex Labs

Working part-time as an embedded systems developer allows me to study industrial engineering workflows directly. This role challenges me to balance theory with factory floor constraints, client hardware parameters, and production-ready code validation cycles.

SLRC Robotics Team

As part of a dedicated student group representing CGTTI Moratuwa, I co-designed and implemented high-speed autonomous control systems for the Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge. This involved rapid iteration, structural chassis adjustments, and robust tracking algorithms built under tight event deadlines.

Academic Track & Goals

My work at CGTTI forms the starting point for a deeper technical trajectory. I am actively mapping out an academic path targeting a B.Tech and an eventual PhD focused on advanced robotics control networks. To lay the foundation for international research environments, I maintain a regular self-study routine targeting the German language.
